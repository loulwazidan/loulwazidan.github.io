LOULWA ZIDAN — PORTFOLIO WEBSITE

index.html is the home page. Every page picks the desktop or phone layout
automatically based on screen size.

TO PUT IT ONLINE (free, about 2 minutes)
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign up / log in.
3. Drag the whole "loulwa-zidan-portfolio" folder onto the page.
4. Netlify gives you a link like something.netlify.app — that's your live site.
   In Site settings you can rename it, or connect your own domain.

To update later: make your changes, then drag the folder onto the same
Netlify site's "Deploys" tab.

Pages: index (home), projects, about, contact, and one page per project.
All images and videos are in the assets folder.
